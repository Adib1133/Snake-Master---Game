How to Play Snake Master

  • Goal: Eat food to grow and rack up points
  • Controls: Arrow Keys or WASD to move · Space / P to pause
  • 🍎 Red food: +10 pts — standard
  • 🌟 Golden food: +50 pts — bonus points
  • ⚡ Cyan food: +15 pts + speed boost
  • 🔥 Mega food: +100 pts — rare jackpot!
  • ☠️ Poison: Shrinks snake & loses points
  • 💛 Heart: Extra life loot drop
  • 🛡️ Shield: Brief invincibility loot
  • 2️⃣ 2×Points: Double points for 8 s loot
  • 📉 Shrink: Trap loot — shortens your snake
  • 🐌 Slow Field: Trap loot — temporary slow-down
  • Combo: Eat fast to build score multiplier
  • Level: Every 50 pts increases speed
  • M key: Toggle sound · ⚙️ Change difficulty
